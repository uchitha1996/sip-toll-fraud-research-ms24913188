## Interpretation and limits

The predeclared prediction held in this selected case: V3 missed the spaced attempts, while V5 identified ten confirmed password rejections before the subsequent call. The apparent V5 detection lead is measured against the first client INVITE, not the moment the call was answered. This is one source, one identity, one destination and one PBX on a LAN. It cannot establish production accuracy, actual credential theft, or a charged outbound toll call. Additional varied normal controls and held-out attack cases are needed before tuning a new progression rule or reporting general precision and recall.
