and reaches its ten-count threshold after five actual wrong-password dialogs.

outbound call succeeds. Neither detector has a distinct COMPROMISE stage or
CDR-assisted call progression rule. These three selected cases use one source,
one identity, one destination, and one PBX; they do not estimate general
precision, recall, a production false-alert rate, or completed toll fraud.
